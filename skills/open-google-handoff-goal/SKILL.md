---
name: open-google-handoff-goal
description: Recover a decision from Gmail, inspect the current linked Drive assets, and leave a verified workspace handoff.
---

Use `reconcileGoogleHandoff(goal)` when the user needs to act on assets linked
from a mail decision. Check who authorized the specific version, account for
later changes or withdrawals, and confirm current Drive evidence appropriate
to the outcome. Save a workspace handoff when requested.
