---
name: open-calendar-goal
description: Reconcile scheduling proposals with current Calendar resource availability and leave a verified workspace handoff.
---

Use `workOnCalendarGoal(goal)` when a user needs a scheduling decision from
proposal and resource calendars. Calendar records can change between pages
and between window review and handoff. Check the requested window, reconcile
the current proposals and conflicts, and verify any selected event by its
provider identity. A handoff records the result in the workspace.
